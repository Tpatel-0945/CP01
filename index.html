          Habits that help

          • Pay yourself first. Move your saving amount aside the day pocket money arrives, then spend from what's left.
          • Use a 24-hour rule for wants. If it isn't a need, wait a day. Most impulse buys lose their pull.
          • Watch the small, frequent ones. Five snacks a week often costs more than one big purchase.
          • Give every category a limit. Start with last month's spending and trim 10% from your top category.
          • Check in weekly. Open the Stats tab every Sunday to see if your pace is on track.
